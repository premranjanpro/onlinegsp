FAQ — Admissions, Courses, Certification & Careers

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

  • Who is GURUKUL — The School of Professionals?

    GURUKUL — The School of Professionals is an Indian, ISO 9001 certified institute that provides professional, vocational, technical and management training. Our head office is in the National Capital Region and we operate regional offices and learning centres across multiple states. We partner with trainers, industry experts and local centres to deliver high-quality, career-focused education.

  • What is the organisation’s main objective?

    Our primary objective is to bring world-class computer education and skill training to learners at their doorstep. We focus on practical, employment-oriented courses and work with industry professionals to ensure our curriculum and training meet employer expectations. We also support entrepreneurship and self-employment through targeted programs.

  • Does GURUKUL have a quality certification?

    Yes — GURUKUL is ISO certified (previously listed as ISO 9001:2008 and updated references to current certification where applicable). The certification demonstrates our commitment to quality processes in training delivery and student services.

  • Will I get a job after completing a course?

    Many students who complete our career-oriented programs find job placements or start successful self-employment ventures. While ability, skills and personality determine placement outcomes, we maintain industry-standard training and provide placement assistance, recruiter connections and guidance to increase employability.

  • Why is service tax charged on fees?

    Service tax or GST on training fees is applied according to applicable government regulations. Charging tax demonstrates compliance and the legal standing of the institute. For current tax rules and rates, please refer to official government notifications or contact our administrative office.

  • Is Gurukul an Indian organisation?

    Yes. Gurukul is an Indian organisation. While we engage with global best practices in IT education, the company is Indian-owned and operated.

  • Do any courses provide self-employment opportunities?

    Yes — courses such as Accounting, DTP, Web Designing, Hardware & Networking and many vocational programs are designed to equip students for self-employment. Thousands of alumni run profitable small businesses after completing such courses.

  • I am weak in English — can I learn computers?

    Absolutely. Our course materials use simple English and many programs are offered with instruction in regional languages. With basic guidance, a little practice and our optional English support classes, most students quickly become comfortable with the language used in computing.

  • How can I verify the genuineness of my admission?

    Ways to verify your admission:

    • Within a few days you receive a registration confirmation accessible from your student login.
    • Your identity card is issued by the centre and dispatched by headquarters.
    • Check your admission details on the official website using your login credentials.
    • Course materials are provided only to admitted students through authorized centres.

  • Is there a minimum qualification to enroll?

    For basic computer literacy courses there is no age or education restriction. Certain career-oriented programs may have specific minimum qualifications which will be listed on the course page. If you are unsure, contact the centre for guidance.

  • Can a regular university student simultaneously study at Gurukul?

    Yes. A student enrolled in a regular university program may simultaneously take technical or vocational courses at our institute, subject to the rules of their university. This practice is generally accepted and legal.

  • When do academic sessions start?

    We operate on an Open Session System (OSS). You can enroll at any time — courses begin as per your enrolment and centre schedule.

If you need further assistance, or to verify specific admission details, please contact your local Gurukul centre or visit the official website.

About Us

Gurukul the School of Professionals is an ISO 9001:2015 certified institute founded to deliver industry-focused education and training across Professional, Vocational, Technical, and Management disciplines. We are committed to providing modern, practical learning that prepares students for real careers.

Our goal is to build a strong nationwide network so quality training reaches learners across India � urban and rural alike. We currently operate offices in Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand, and we partner with trusted members and centers in many other states.

We build local partnerships and work with community champions to promote programs at the grassroots level. Our emphasis is on skill proficiency, employability, and connecting training to local opportunities�bridging modern techniques with local contexts.
