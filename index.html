About Us

Gurukul the School of Professionals is an ISO 9001:2015 certified institute founded to deliver industry-focused education and training across Professional, Vocational, Technical, and Management disciplines. We are committed to providing modern, practical learning that prepares students for real careers.

Our goal is to build a strong nationwide network so quality training reaches learners across India � urban and rural alike. We currently operate offices in Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand, and we partner with trusted members and centers in many other states.

We build local partnerships and work with community champions to promote programs at the grassroots level. Our emphasis is on skill proficiency, employability, and connecting training to local opportunities�bridging modern techniques with local contexts.
