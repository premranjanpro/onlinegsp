Certificate & Marksheet Verification Guide

Certificate verification helps a candidate, employer or institution compare a document with the record available in an online verification system. It is useful when a document contains a QR code or unique verification reference.

What to check

  • Candidate name and course
  • Roll or enrollment number
  • Issue date and examination information
  • Marks or result status where displayed
  • QR code or verification reference

How to verify

Open the Certificate Verification page and enter the requested information or scan the document's QR code when supported. Compare the displayed record with the physical or digital document.

If details do not match

Do not assume that a mismatch is proof of fraud. Check the identifier again and contact the issuing institution or support desk for clarification and correction procedures.

For employers and institutions

Use verification as one part of your document-checking process. For high-stakes decisions, request original documentation and follow your organization's verification requirements.
